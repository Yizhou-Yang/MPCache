\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Video Diffusion Transformers (DiTs) synthesize coherent multi-second clips by integrating a guided velocity field across $T \approx 50$ sequential steps \citep{peebles2023dit,yang2025cogvideox,wan2025wan,kong2024hunyuanvideo}. Under classifier-free guidance (CFG) \citep{ho2022cfg}, every step executes two multi-billion-parameter passes over tens of thousands of spatiotemporal tokens, making sampling latency the primary deployment bottleneck. Training-free caching removes redundant evaluations without retraining weights or altering solver grids: $B < T$ anchor steps run the exact DiT, while the remaining $T - B$ steps synthesize velocities from cached anchors \citep{ma2024deepcache,liu2025teacache,liu2025taylorseer,liu2026racfgcache}.

Despite rapid empirical progress, existing works design step selection (\emph{when to compute}) and cache reconstruction (\emph{what to return}) as heuristic, independent modules, leaving three striking contradictions in the literature unresolved:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The Predictor Sign-Inversion Paradox:} Multi-step feature forecasters such as TaylorSeer \citep{liu2025taylorseer} advocate linear or polynomial extrapolation, whereas risk-aware caches \citep{liu2025teacache,liu2026racfgcache} revert to plain zeroth-order reuse ($\tilde{v}_j = v_s$) after finding that extrapolation degrades video quality. Why does the \emph{exact same} first-order chord degrade terminal fidelity by $+31\%$ on threshold schedules yet slash perceptual error by $33\text{--}42\%$ once early steps are locked?
\item \textbf{The Input-Observability \& Prior-Inversion Trap:} Threshold caches trigger refreshes from modulated-input deltas \citep{liu2025teacache}, local Jacobians \citep{haghighi2026sencache}, or open-loop solver coefficients, while offline planners \citep{cui2026dpcache} collect $\mathcal{O}(N_{\mathrm{cal}} T^2)$ pairwise tensors with hard-coded $M=3$ prefixes. Why does triggering on TeaCache's input proxy underperform output-drift scheduling by \textbf{$4.44\times$ at the exact same 19 calls}, and why do open-loop solver priors \emph{invert Spearman rank correlation} ($\rho: +0.55 \to -0.15$) as skip gaps widen?
\item \textbf{Fixed-Budget Feedback vs.\ Quality Certification:} Online controllers expend runtime overhead adapting schedules per prompt, yet lack any pre-generation quality guarantee. When does online feedback provably collapse to a zero-overhead offline schedule, and how can a cache solve the \emph{inverse} problem---synthesizing the minimum call count $B^\star(\eps, \delta)$ certified to satisfy a user perceptual bound $\eps$ on unseen prompts?
\end{enumerate}

In this paper, we show that these paradoxes are manifestations of four sharp structural laws of guided DiT trajectories---and that \emph{every law directly dictates a principled algorithmic operator} without modifying model weights or solver grids. Formulating caching as a coupled bilevel operator-design problem, we introduce \textbf{\method{}} (Figures~\ref{fig:teaser}--\ref{fig:arch}), advancing both the scientific understanding and algorithmic toolkit of diffusion caching:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Four Trace-Verified Structural Laws \& Operator Non-Commutativity (Section~\ref{sec:formulation}, Proposition~\ref{prop:subspace}):} Unrolling the closed-loop guided adjoint $\Psi_{k\to T}$ across $>2{,}600$ GPU trajectory runs uncovers: (i)~a \emph{two-regime dynamical bifurcation} from $84.5^\circ$ orthogonal chord rotation ($\cos\theta_5 = 0.096$) and peak amplification ($\norm{\Psi_{3\to T}} = 0.963$) to coherent $8.1\times$-contracted transport ($\cos\theta_{23} = 0.499$), proving that scheduling and prediction \emph{do not commute}; (ii)~a $4.44\times$ input-proxy observability gap alongside terminal solver-bookkeeping collapse ($0.449 \to 0.072$ rel-$L_2$ on UniPC when $T-1$ is unpinned); (iii)~prompt-invariant worst-to-mean scaling ($\max/\text{mean} = 1.63$, $\text{CV} = 1.04\%$); and (iv)~cross-patch phase tearing under hidden-feature polynomials ($0.492$ LPIPS) and per-token/low-rank channel gates ($12\%$ win rate).
\item \textbf{Boundary-Pinned Diffusive Bellman DP \& Stiff-Sloppy Transfer (Section~\ref{sec:dp}, Theorem~\ref{thm:kkt}, Corollary~\ref{cor:sloppy}):} From one $\mathcal{O}(T)$ terminal impulse sweep, our parameter-free diffusive age functional $\cC_{0.5}(\cS)$ raises cross-budget LPIPS prediction from $R^2=0.835$ to $\mathbf{0.985}$ ($6.50$ vs.\ $6.56$ at 5 skips). Global Bellman DP and KKT analysis prove budget-expanding head saturation $H^\star(B)$ ($H^\star(22)=16$) and an \textbf{$11.6\times$ stiff-sloppy curvature anisotropy}: performance is stiff at boundaries $(H^\star, T-1)$ ($+178.5\%$ loss when halving $H$) yet spectrally flat across interior tail ratios $r \in [1.4, 3.0]$ ($<1.6\%$ LPIPS spread), enabling zero-shot schedule transfer from CogVideoX-2B (DDIM) to Wan2.1-1.3B (2nd-order UniPC).
\item \textbf{Scale-Invariance Collapse \& Conformal Budget Certification (Section~\ref{sec:invariance}, Theorems~\ref{thm:invariance}--\ref{thm:certify}):} We prove that under fixed budget $B$, multiplicative prompt difficulty cancels out of receding-horizon MPC ($0/50$ step changes across 336 runs). Conversely, exploiting quantile invariance over the $<1.5$~ms $\mathcal{O}(T^3)$ Bellman Pareto table yields \texttt{CertifyBudget}, a distribution-free controller returning the minimum call budget $B^\star(\eps, \delta, \alpha)$ guaranteed to bound mean or worst-case LPIPS---automatically converting inner shrinkage into \textbf{$4\text{--}6$ fewer DiT calls ($19\text{--}24\%$ compute savings)} at identical certified quality.
\item \textbf{Phase-Gated 2-Slot Midpoint Chord-Shrinkage (Section~\ref{sec:shrinkage}, Theorem~\ref{thm:shrinkage}):} Operating in spatially coherent output-tangent space with only 2 cached tensors ($30\text{--}60\times$ less VRAM than hidden-layer Taylor buffers), $\tilde{v}_t = v_s + \alpha\,\mathbb{I}[t \ge H_{\mathrm{crit}}](v_s - v_{s'})$ obeys an exact Hilbert quadratic law ($R^2 = 0.9974$) minimized near $\alpha^\star \approx 1/2$, eliminating first-order quadrature drift without $\Theta(g^3)$ curvature blow-up ($12.39\times$) while gating out $+31\%$ early-regime noise under ultra-low budgets.
\end{itemize}
