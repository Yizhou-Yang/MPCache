\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Video Diffusion Transformers (DiTs) synthesize high-fidelity multi-second clips by integrating a guided velocity field across $T \approx 50$ sequential solver steps \citep{peebles2023dit,yang2025cogvideox,wan2025wan,kong2024hunyuanvideo}. Under classifier-free guidance (CFG) \citep{ho2022cfg}, each step executes two multi-billion-parameter passes over tens of thousands of spatiotemporal tokens, making sampling latency the primary bottleneck in deployment. Training-free caching accelerates inference without retraining weights or altering solver grids: $B < T$ anchor steps evaluate the exact DiT, while the remaining $T - B$ steps synthesize velocities from cached anchors \citep{ma2024deepcache,liu2025teacache,liu2025taylorseer,liu2026racfgcache}.

Despite rapid progress, existing training-free caches lack a unified operator-design methodology, leaving three fundamental **algorithmic gaps**:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Myopic Surrogates vs.\ Costly Rollout Tensors:} Online threshold caches \citep{liu2025teacache,haghighi2026sencache,liu2026racfgcache} decide step-by-step from local input deltas, local Jacobians, or instantaneous branch risk, ignoring how perturbations compound through $T - 1 - j$ subsequent closed-loop solver-denoiser transitions. Conversely, offline path planners \citep{cui2026dpcache,xue2026idealcache} require $\mathcal{O}(N_{\mathrm{cal}} T^2)$ pairwise rollout evaluations and hard-code ad-hoc prefixes (e.g., $M=3$) without an analytical theory of horizon allocation or cross-backbone transfer.
\item \textbf{Uncertified Budgets vs.\ Redundant Online Loops:} Prior controllers either fix an arbitrary call count $B$ or expend runtime overhead adapting thresholds per prompt without any distribution-free perceptual guarantee. A principled control theory must characterize when online trajectory feedback is mathematically invariant under fixed $B$, and solve the \emph{inverse} problem: synthesizing the minimum call budget $B^\star(\eps, \delta)$ certified to bound perceptual distortion $\eps$ on unseen prompts.
\item \textbf{Unbounded Extrapolation Variance \& Cache Footprint:} Multi-step feature forecasters such as TaylorSeer \citep{liu2025taylorseer} store $L(O{+}1)$ hidden-layer tensors and scale finite differences by step age $(j - s)^p$, amplifying unmeasured manifold curvature and often degrading fidelity below plain zeroth-order reuse ($\alpha = 0$).
\end{enumerate}

In this paper, we address all three methodological challenges by formulating training-free video diffusion caching as a coupled \emph{bilevel variational operator-synthesis and risk-certification problem} (Figures~\ref{fig:teaser}--\ref{fig:arch}). We propose \textbf{\method{}}, a complete zero-training framework that first establishes closed-form and dynamic-programming synthesis rules for outer scheduling, conformal budget certification, and inner tangent shrinkage (Section~\ref{sec:method}), and then leverages this operator theory to uncover four sharp mechanistic discoveries that resolve open contradictions across the literature (Section~\ref{sec:discoveries}):
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Outer Diffusive Bellman Synthesis \& Stiff-Sloppy Transfer (Section~\ref{sec:dp}, Proposition~\ref{prop:subspace}, Theorem~\ref{thm:kkt}, Corollary~\ref{cor:sloppy}):} Unrolling the closed-loop guided adjoint $\Psi_{k\to T}$, we identify terminal sensitivities in one $\mathcal{O}(T)$ pass and solve a parameter-free diffusive age-compounding Bellman DP ($\beta=1/2$) in $<1.5$~ms. KKT and Bregman analysis prove budget-expanding head saturation $H^\star(B)$ ($H^\star(22)=16$) alongside an \emph{anisotropic stiff-sloppy hierarchy}---first-order stiff at $(H^\star, T-1)$ yet second-order sloppy wrt tail decay---guaranteeing zero-shot schedule transfer across backbones and solver orders.
\item \textbf{MPC Collapse \& Conformal Budget Certification (Section~\ref{sec:invariance}, Theorems~\ref{thm:invariance}--\ref{thm:certify}):} We prove that under fixed budget $B$, multiplicative prompt drift cancels out of receding-horizon control, collapsing online MPC to our zero-overhead offline schedule. Conversely, we introduce \texttt{CertifyBudget}, a split-conformal controller over the $\mathcal{O}(T^3)$ Bellman Pareto table that synthesizes the minimum certified budget $B^\star(\eps, \delta, \alpha)$ and converts inner shrinkage into $5\text{--}6$ saved DiT calls ($19\text{--}21\%$ compute savings).
\item \textbf{Phase-Gated 2-Slot Midpoint Shrinkage (Section~\ref{sec:shrinkage}, Theorem~\ref{thm:shrinkage}, Algorithm~\ref{alg:mpcache}):} We introduce the 2-slot guided velocity operator $\tilde{v}_t = v_s + \alpha\,\mathbb{I}[t \ge H_{\mathrm{crit}}](v_s - v_{s'})$ and prove that squared perceptual error in VGG Hilbert space obeys an exact convex parabola ($R^2 = 0.9974$) minimized near $\alpha^\star \approx 1/2$---canceling first-order solver drift without $\Theta(g^3)$ Taylor blow-up, gating out early noise under ultra-low budgets, and cutting cache VRAM by $30\text{--}60\times$.
\item \textbf{Four Sharp Mechanistic Discoveries (Section~\ref{sec:discoveries}):} Diagnosing $>2{,}600$ GPU trajectories via \method{}'s operators uncovers: (1)~a \emph{predictor sign-inversion law} ($+31\%$ harm vs.\ $-42\%$ gain) driven by $84.5^\circ$ Regime-I chord orthogonality and $8.1\times$ Jacobian contraction; (2)~a $4.44\times$ input-proxy observability gap and multi-step prior rank inversion ($\rho: +0.55 \to -0.15$); (3)~$1.04\%$-CV cross-prompt quantile invariance ($R^2 = 0.985$ vs.\ $0.835$ additive); and (4)~3D-VAE spatial phase tearing under hidden polynomials ($4.72\times$ worse LPIPS) and token/channel gates.
\end{itemize}
