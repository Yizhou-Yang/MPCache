# Brief for the illustrator — MPCache figures

Three things must stay exact, because they are measurements: **the numbers, the step positions,
and the ordering**. Everything else is free.

## 1. Teaser (Figure 1) — the one that needs the most work

Today it is four charts in four boxes, which reads as a graphic abstract rather than a teaser.
The redesign should be **image led**, in the spirit of a benchmark-example figure: one real
video strip with callouts, plus two small evidence insets.

**Composition**

- A horizontal filmstrip of four to six frames from one generated clip, full width.
- Over the strip, a thin timeline from step 0 to step 49 with 22 marks: sixteen packed at the
  left, six spread to the right. This is the schedule and it is exact:
  `0..15, 18, 22, 28, 35, 42, 49`.
- Two callouts on the timeline. Left, over the packed head: "an error here is carried by every
  later step". Right, over a sparse gap: "an error here is re-explained before the decode".
- Bottom left inset, small: the cost profile, with only two numbers printed, `0.57` at step 1 and
  `0.04` after step 10.
- Bottom right inset, small: three bars, `0.155` reuse, `0.104` damped, `0.442` scaled by the gap.

**What you may change**: the strip's framing (polaroid, film sprockets, rounded cards), the
arrow and callout style, the palette within the paper's four colours, the typography of the
labels.

**What you may not change**: the 22 positions, the three bar values, the two profile numbers,
and the fact that the profile is dark at the left and flat at the right.

## 2. The mechanism figure (Figure 4, `fig_mechanism.pdf`) — now two panels, not one sketch

The method section now derives the damping, it does not just measure it (see the minimax
paragraph in `method.tex`, Eq.~3): the two stored outputs cannot tell the pipeline whether the
trend that produced $\Delta$ is about to continue ($\lambda=1$, "persist") or stop outright
($\lambda=0$, "reuse"), and the worst-case-optimal guess over that interval is $\alpha=\tfrac12$.
A first working draft exists at `fig_mechanism.py` / `fig_mechanism.pdf` (matplotlib, house
colours and sizes already applied); treat it as the content specification, not the final art.

**Panel (a), illustrative, free to restyle.**
- Two grey dots on a gently curving path: the previous exact step $v_{s'}$ and the anchor $v_s$.
- A short red arrow from the anchor landing near the true continuation, marked ✓, labelled
  "damped, $\alpha=\tfrac12$".
- One long grey arrow in the same direction, with a tick where it passes "persist, $\alpha=1$"
  and a cross where it ends, labelled "scaled by the gap".
- The anchor itself is also labelled "reuse, $\alpha=0$": it is the *other* worst-case point, not
  just the first marker on the path.

**Keep**: three named points on the grey arrow's line — reuse ($\alpha=0$) at the anchor, persist
($\alpha=1$) as a waypoint tick, and the gap-scaled failure as the arrow's end — plus the damped
arrow landing closest to the true curve. **Free**: how the path is drawn (river, rope, smooth
curve), and the exact curvature.

**Panel (b), a data/derived-function panel, do not redraw beyond colour.** It plots the exact
worst-case risk $\max(\alpha^2,(1-\alpha)^2)$ against $\alpha$: this is the minimax derivation,
not a sketch, and belongs in the "never redraw" list below alongside `fig_damping.pdf` and
`fig_sensitivity.pdf`. The only things worth polishing are typography and the two endpoint labels
("reuse" at $\alpha=0$, "undamped" at $\alpha=1$), which must stay at height 1 ($\Vert\Delta\Vert^2$).

## 3. Architecture (Figure 2)

The boxes are fine; the look is not. It should read as one pipeline, not two rows of rectangles.

- Band A, "once per sampler": ablation → profile → allocation → schedule.
- Band B, "once per video": step → decide → call or reuse → decode, with the loop back.
- Icons are welcome (a small transformer block, a cache slot, a decoder); text must stay verbatim.

## Never redraw these

`fig_sensitivity.pdf` and `fig_damping.pdf` are data figures generated by scripts. So is panel
(b) of `fig_mechanism.pdf` (the minimax risk curve) — it is an exact closed-form function, not a
measurement, but the same rule applies. All three must stay vector plots with the exact numbers
and curves; an illustrator may only adjust colour and spacing. Panel (a) of `fig_mechanism.pdf` is
the one exception inside that file: it is illustrative and may be fully restyled, subject to the
"keep" list in section 2 above.

## House style

Two-column figure, 7.0 in wide, height at most 2.4 in. Axis labels 7.5 pt, tick labels 7 pt,
annotations 6.5 pt. Four colours only: `#c0504d` (ours), `#2f6fb3` (measurement), `#7f7f7f`
(the failed variant), `#cfd6dd` (rules and guides). No gradients, no drop shadows, no 3D.

## Deliverables

PDF vector plus the source file (`.ai`, `.fig`, or a script). Every number in the figure must be
traceable to `sourcecode/scripts/latest/` or `experiments_results/main_table.json`, except panel
(b) of `fig_mechanism.pdf`, whose curve is traceable instead to Eq.~3 in `method.tex` (the
minimax derivation) rather than to a measurement file.
