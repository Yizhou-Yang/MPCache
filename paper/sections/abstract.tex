Training-free caching accelerates video Diffusion Transformers (DiTs) by evaluating $B < T$ anchor steps and reconstructing intermediate velocities, yet prior methods rely on decoupled heuristics---pairing myopic local thresholds or costly $\mathcal{O}(N_{\mathrm{cal}} T^2)$ rollout tensors with plain reuse or unstable polynomial extrapolation, without horizon optimality or error guarantees. We introduce \textbf{\method{}}, a principled \emph{bilevel variational operator-synthesis and risk-certification framework} for training-free video diffusion caching. At the outer level, we linearize the closed-loop guided solver-denoiser horizon (\textbf{Proposition~1}), derive a parameter-free diffusive age-compounding functional from a single $\mathcal{O}(T)$ impulse pass, solve the global Pareto schedule frontier in $<1.5$~ms via boundary-pinned Bellman dynamic programming (\textbf{Theorem~1}), prove fixed-budget receding-horizon MPC collapse (\textbf{Theorem~2}), and introduce \texttt{CertifyBudget} (\textbf{Theorem~3}), a conformal controller returning the minimum call budget $B^\star(\eps, \delta)$ certified to satisfy user perceptual bounds. At the inner level, we propose a 2-slot \emph{phase-gated midpoint chord-shrinkage} operator in guided velocity tangent space (\textbf{Theorem~4}), minimizing strictly convex Hilbert quadratic risk ($R^2 = 0.997$) without $\Theta(g^3)$ curvature blow-up at $30\text{--}60\times$ lower cache memory and zero extra FLOPs. Applying our operator framework to $>2{,}600$ GPU trajectories further uncovers four sharp mechanistic discoveries that resolve contradictions in prior work: (1)~a \emph{predictor sign-inversion law} ($+31\%$ harm vs.\ $-42\%$ gain) driven by an $84.5^\circ$ two-regime bifurcation; (2)~an $11.6\times$ \emph{stiff-sloppy KKT hierarchy} (\textbf{Corollary~1}) enabling zero-shot cross-backbone schedule transfer while exposing a $4.44\times$ input-proxy observability gap; (3)~$1.04\%$-CV cross-prompt quantile invariance converting midpoint shrinkage into $5\text{--}6$ saved DiT calls; and (4)~3D-VAE spatial phase coherence beating hidden Taylor polynomials ($4.72\times$) and per-token/channel gates. Across CogVideoX-2B and Wan2.1-1.3B on 100 VBench prompts, \method{} consistently outperforms TeaCache, TaylorSeer, and RA-CFGCache while cutting remaining LPIPS by $32.8\%$ ($+3.70$~dB PSNR).
