\section{Discussion, Scope, and Conclusion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{From Empirical Anomalies to Operator Synthesis.}
Our findings resolve several longstanding contradictions in diffusion caching by showing that they follow deterministically from closed-loop operator geometry. First, whether velocity extrapolation helps or hurts is governed by \emph{non-commutativity across the dynamical bifurcation $H_{\mathrm{crit}}$}: before $H_{\mathrm{crit}}$, $84.5^\circ$ chord rotation turns extrapolation into $+31\%$ orthogonal noise amplified by $\Psi_{i\to T}$, whereas locking $[0, H^\star(B))$ via Bellman DP flips the exact same chord into a $33\text{--}42\%$ error reducer (and \texttt{RegimeGate} protects ultra-low budgets when $B \le H_{\mathrm{crit}}$). Second, the $11.6\times$ stiff-sloppy curvature hierarchy explains why schedules require exact head saturation and terminal solver pinning ($T-1 \in \cS^\star$) yet transfer zero-shot across distinct video architectures and ODE/flow-matching solver orders. Third, spatial phase coherence explains why a 2-slot global output-tangent scalar beats both $L(O{+}1)$ hidden-feature polynomials and per-token/low-rank channel maps at $30\text{--}60\times$ lower memory.

\paragraph{Fixed-Budget Invariance vs.\ Risk-Certified Adaptive Stopping.}
Theorems~\ref{thm:invariance} and~\ref{thm:certify} draw a sharp boundary between wasteful and productive adaptivity. Under a fixed call budget $B$, prompt-level difficulty scales drift multiplicatively and cancels out of Bellman optimization ($0/50$ step changes). Productive adaptivity instead operates at the \emph{budget-certification} level: \texttt{CertifyBudget} exploits $1.04\%$-CV quantile invariance and $R^2=0.985$ diffusive Bellman scaling to convert inner midpoint shrinkage directly into $4\text{--}6$ fewer DiT evaluations under a distribution-free perceptual guarantee.

\paragraph{Limitations and Future Work.}
First, while Corollary~\ref{cor:sloppy} enables zero-shot transfer across 50-step video backbones with comparable bifurcation fractions, changing grid length $T$ substantially (e.g., few-step distilled samplers) requires running the one-time $\mathcal{O}(T)$ single-skip sweep on one calibration prompt. Second, below $B < 10$ of $50$ steps, even Regime~I cannot be fully locked; \texttt{RegimeGate} prevents $+31\%$ extrapolation blow-up, but training-free skipping ultimately meets the subspace floor without distillation. Extending certified horizon-and-shrinkage synthesis to spatiotemporal token routing \citep{zou2024toca,feng2026worldcache} is a natural next step.
