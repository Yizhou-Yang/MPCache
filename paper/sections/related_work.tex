\section{Related Work and Operator Taxonomy}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Sampling Acceleration and Caching Granularities}
Accelerating diffusion sampling follows two complementary axes. Trajectory-compression methods design high-order ODE/SDE solvers \citep{song2021ddim,lu2022dpm,zhao2023unipc} or distill teachers into few-step generators \citep{salimans2022progressive,song2023consistency,meng2023lcm}, requiring post-training compute and risking motion loss under strong CFG \citep{yang2025cogvideox,wan2025wan,kong2024hunyuanvideo,zheng2024opensora}. Training-free caching preserves pretrained weights and native solver grids, reusing computations across blocks \citep{ma2024deepcache,chen2024ddit}, attention/CFG branches \citep{zhao2024pab,lv2024fastercache}, spatiotemporal tokens \citep{zou2024toca,feng2026worldcache}, or full-forward DiT steps \citep{liu2025teacache,liu2025taylorseer,liu2026racfgcache}. Full-step caching delivers architecture-agnostic speedups proportional to $T / B$ without custom sparse kernels, and is the focus of our methodology.

\subsection{Methodological Axes of Full-Step Caching}
Figure~\ref{fig:map} and Table~\ref{tab:taxonomy} organize full-step DiT caching methods along our outer and inner operator axes.

\paragraph{Horizon Placement and Certification.}
TeaCache \citep{liu2025teacache} accumulates rescaled modulated-input deltas; SenCache \citep{haghighi2026sencache} bounds local denoiser Jacobians ($\partial_x v_\theta, \partial_t v_\theta$); RA-CFGCache \citep{liu2026racfgcache} tracks dual-branch CFG risk; and RACER \citep{li2026racer} uses forecaster disagreement, while learned routers \citep{ma2024learning} require training. Because local triggers ignore closed-loop horizon contraction and boundary pinning, they under-allocate the head transition or exhaust budgets prematurely. Global planners such as DPCache \citep{cui2026dpcache} and IdealCache \citep{xue2026idealcache} cast selection as DP or shortest-path search, but DPCache requires an $\mathcal{O}(N_{\mathrm{cal}} T^2)$ pairwise tensor and hard-codes $M=3$ initial steps, while neither certifies user error targets or guarantees cross-backbone transfer. \method{} synthesizes global schedules from one $\mathcal{O}(T)$ impulse pass, proves $H^\star(B)$ scaling and stiff-sloppy transfer, proves fixed-budget MPC collapse \citep{rawlings2017mpc}, and introduces conformal minimum-budget synthesis $B^\star(\eps, \delta)$.

\paragraph{Velocity Reconstruction.}
Threshold and graph planners default to zeroth-order reuse ($\alpha=0$), whereas TaylorSeer \citep{liu2025taylorseer} caches $O{+}1$ finite-difference tensors in every transformer block ($L(O{+}1)$ buffers) and scales updates by $(j - s)^p$. Our methodology replaces multi-layer gap-scaled polynomials with 2-slot phase-gated midpoint chord-shrinkage in guided output-tangent space, eliminating $\Theta(g^3)$ variance inflation at zero extra FLOPs.
