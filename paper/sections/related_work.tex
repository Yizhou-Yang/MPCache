\section{Related Work and Operator Taxonomy}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Sampling Acceleration and Caching Granularities}
Accelerating diffusion inference broadly follows two orthogonal axes. Trajectory-shortening methods redesign numerical ODE/SDE integrators \citep{song2021ddim,lu2022dpm,zhao2023unipc} or distill many-step teachers into few-step generators \citep{salimans2022progressive,song2023consistency,meng2023lcm}. While effective, distillation requires costly post-training and can degrade motion diversity under high CFG in video generation \citep{yang2025cogvideox,wan2025wan,kong2024hunyuanvideo,zheng2024opensora}. Training-free caching preserves pretrained weights and native solver grids, skipping redundant computation at the granularity of transformer blocks \citep{ma2024deepcache,chen2024ddit}, attention maps and CFG branches \citep{zhao2024pab,lv2024fastercache}, spatial-temporal tokens \citep{zou2024toca,feng2026worldcache}, or full-forward denoiser steps \citep{liu2025teacache,liu2025taylorseer,liu2026racfgcache}. Full-step caching delivers architecture-agnostic speedups proportional to $T / B$ without custom sparse kernels, and forms the focus of our framework.

\subsection{Placement and Reconstruction Operators}
Figure~\ref{fig:map} and Table~\ref{tab:taxonomy} organize modern full-step DiT caches along the two coupled operators formalized in this work.

\paragraph{Local Thresholds vs.\ Global Horizon Planning.}
TeaCache \citep{liu2025teacache} accumulates rescaled modulated-input differences; SenCache \citep{haghighi2026sencache} formalizes local denoiser sensitivity via input/timestep Jacobians ($\partial_x v_\theta, \partial_t v_\theta$) calibrated on 8 videos; RA-CFGCache \citep{liu2026racfgcache} composes dual-branch CFG risk weighted by an offline prior; and RACER \citep{li2026racer} triggers refreshes from forecaster disagreement. Because local rules price perturbations myopically or exhaust budget prematurely when early inputs move rapidly, they systematically under-protect the late-head transition, while learned routers \citep{ma2024learning} leave the training-free setting. Concurrent global planners---DPCache \citep{cui2026dpcache} and IdealCache \citep{xue2026idealcache}---cast step selection as dynamic programming or shortest-path search. However, DPCache requires an expensive $\mathcal{O}(N_{\mathrm{cal}} T^2)$ pairwise rollout tensor and hard-codes the first $M=3$ steps without analyzing head scaling, whereas IdealCache optimizes intermediate Euclidean distance to a straight-line interpolation rather than decoded perceptual distortion. In contrast, \method{} needs only one $\mathcal{O}(T)$ terminal perceptual impulse pass, derives super-additive interval costs from anchor-age drift, proves the KKT head-saturation law ($H^\star(22)=16$), and establishes when online receding-horizon MPC \citep{rawlings2017mpc} collapses to offline Bellman optimality.

\paragraph{Zeroth-Order Reuse vs.\ Extrapolated Reconstruction.}
On skipped steps, threshold and graph-planning caches return the stored output unchanged ($\alpha=0$). TaylorSeer \citep{liu2025taylorseer} and DPCache's predictive variant instead extrapolate cached features or outputs via gap-scaled finite-difference Taylor polynomials, scaling step differences by $(j - s)^p$. As Theorem~\ref{thm:shrinkage} proves, two noisy anchors on a curved 50-step video trajectory determine a local chord direction but cannot support unbounded gap scaling without inflating unmeasured curvature by $\mathcal{O}(g^3)$. Our midpoint chord-shrinkage operator resolves this bias-variance trade-off in closed form.
