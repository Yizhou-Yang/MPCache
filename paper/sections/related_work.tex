\section{Related Work and Operator Taxonomy}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Sampling Acceleration and Caching Granularities}
Accelerating diffusion sampling follows two complementary directions. Trajectory-compression methods design high-order ODE/SDE solvers \citep{song2021ddim,lu2022dpm,zhao2023unipc} or distill teachers into few-step generators \citep{salimans2022progressive,song2023consistency,meng2023lcm}, at the cost of post-training compute and potential motion degradation under strong CFG \citep{yang2025cogvideox,wan2025wan,kong2024hunyuanvideo,zheng2024opensora}. Training-free caching preserves pretrained weights and native solver grids, reusing computations across blocks \citep{ma2024deepcache,chen2024ddit}, attention/CFG branches \citep{zhao2024pab,lv2024fastercache}, spatiotemporal tokens \citep{zou2024toca,feng2026worldcache}, or full-forward DiT steps \citep{liu2025teacache,liu2025taylorseer,liu2026racfgcache}. Full-step caching achieves wall-clock speedups proportional to $T / B$ without custom sparse kernels, and is the focus of our framework.

\subsection{Placement, Certification, and Reconstruction}
Figure~\ref{fig:map} and Table~\ref{tab:taxonomy} contrast modern full-step DiT caches across five algorithmic axes.

\paragraph{Myopic Proxies and Pairwise Planners vs.\ Certified Horizon Control.}
TeaCache \citep{liu2025teacache} monitors rescaled modulated-input deltas; SenCache \citep{haghighi2026sencache} bounds local denoiser Jacobians ($\partial_x v_\theta, \partial_t v_\theta$); RA-CFGCache \citep{liu2026racfgcache} tracks dual-branch CFG risk; and RACER \citep{li2026racer} uses forecaster disagreement, while learned routers \citep{ma2024learning} require training. As shown in Section~\ref{sec:laws}, local input/open-loop signals fail to observe closed-loop Jacobian contraction and can exhaust budgets prematurely, leaving terminal blackouts on multistep flow solvers. Offline planners such as DPCache \citep{cui2026dpcache} and IdealCache \citep{xue2026idealcache} use dynamic programming or graph search, but DPCache requires an $\mathcal{O}(N_{\mathrm{cal}} T^2)$ pairwise rollout tensor and hard-codes $M=3$ initial steps, while neither provides distribution-free error certificates or explains cross-backbone schedule transfer. \method{} derives global schedules from one $\mathcal{O}(T)$ impulse pass, proves why $H^\star(B)$ scales with $B$ ($H^\star(22)=16$) while interior tail ratios remain spectrally sloppy, proves fixed-budget MPC collapse \citep{rawlings2017mpc}, and introduces conformal minimum-budget synthesis $B^\star(\eps, \delta)$.

\paragraph{Hidden-Feature Polynomials vs.\ Output-Tangent Shrinkage.}
Threshold and graph caches default to plain reuse ($\alpha=0$), whereas TaylorSeer \citep{liu2025taylorseer} caches $O{+}1$ finite-difference tensors inside every transformer block ($L(O{+}1)$ buffers) and scales updates by $(j - s)^p$. We show that feature-space polynomial forecasting fails due to $\Theta(g^3)$ curvature inflation, inter-block representation rotation, and cross-patch phase incoherence---all resolved by 2-slot phase-gated midpoint shrinkage in guided velocity space.
