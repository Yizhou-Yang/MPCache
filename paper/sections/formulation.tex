\label{sec:discoveries}
\label{sec:formulation}

Beyond verifying every theoretical premise of \method{}, our operator framework uncovers four counter-intuitive discoveries that explain longstanding contradictions in prior caching methods.

\paragraph{Discovery I: Two-Regime Bifurcation \& The Predictor Sign-Inversion Paradox.}
Prior literature disagrees on whether velocity extrapolation helps \citep{liu2025taylorseer} or hurts \citep{liu2025teacache,liu2026racfgcache} video generation. Evaluating $\Psi_{i\to T}$ and chord angles $\cos\theta_i = \langle \Delta v_{i+1}, \Delta v_i\rangle / (\norm{\Delta v_{i+1}}\norm{\Delta v_i})$ across 192 single-skip runs reveals a sharp **two-regime bifurcation** at $H_{\mathrm{crit}}$ ($10\text{--}12$ at $T=30$, $14\text{--}16$ at $T=50$; Figure~\ref{fig:theory}a): in \emph{Regime~I ($i < H_{\mathrm{crit}}$)}, chords rotate nearly orthogonally ($\cos\theta_5 = \mathbf{0.0959}$, $\mathbf{84.5^\circ}$!; innovation $1.088$) while closed-loop gain peaks at $\gamma_3 = \mathbf{0.9630}$; in \emph{Regime~II ($i \ge H_{\mathrm{crit}}$)}, chord alignment recovers $\mathbf{5.20\times}$ ($0.0959 \to 0.4988$) while Jacobian gain contracts $\mathbf{8.11\times}$ ($0.9630 \to 0.1188$; ratio-of-means $6.42\times$), whereas open-loop $c_0$ moves backwards ($0.85\times$). Consequently, $\cS$ and $\cR$ \textbf{do not commute} (Figure~\ref{fig:theory}b): across 576 runs (Appendix~\ref{app:extended_traces}), 1st-order extrapolation \emph{hurts} by $\mathbf{+31.0\%}$ ($0.2810 \to 0.3681$ rel-$L_2$) on head-skipping schedules and uniform grids trap hindsight 3-anchor `lsq` at $0.4218$; yet locking $[0, H_{\mathrm{crit}})$ via Theorem~\ref{thm:kkt} flips the \emph{exact same} chord to $\mathbf{-42.2\%}$ ($0.1568 \to 0.0907$, within $2.6\%$ of hindsight `lsq`).

\paragraph{Discovery II: Input Anti-Observability, Multi-Step Rank Inversion, \& $11.6\times$ Stiff-Sloppy Anisotropy.}
Why do input-threshold and solver-prior rules misallocate calls? Holding scheduler, quota rule, and budget ($B=19$) strictly identical on CogVideoX-2B, TeaCache's modulated-input delta yields $0.2320$ LPIPS vs.\ $\mathbf{0.0523}$ for output drift---a \textbf{$4.44\times$ observability gap} (and our 10-call schedule at $0.1229$ beats 22-call input thresholding at $0.2543$). Under $\omega=6$ CFG, input norms follow open-loop step sizes rather than CFG branch rotation; worse, across 160 multi-gap runs ($g - 1 \in \{1..5\}$), open-loop solver priors \emph{invert Spearman rank correlation} with terminal error from $+0.545$ ($g=2$) to $\mathbf{-0.148}$ ($g=6$), whereas $\cC_{0.5}$ retains $+0.909$ and matches $6.56\times$ 5-skip compounding ($6.50$ vs.\ $4.55$ additive; Figure~\ref{fig:theory}c). Simultaneously, across 699 Wan2.1 UniPC runs, bypassing Eq.~\eqref{eq:solver_step} collapses LPIPS to $0.83$, and omitting terminal pin $T-1$ induces a $4.56\times$ blackout ($0.3295$ vs.\ $0.0723$ rel-$L_2$). Conversely, once boundaries $(H^\star, T-1)$ are respected, Corollary~\ref{cor:sloppy}'s **stiff-sloppy hierarchy** emerges: halving $H = 12 \to 6$ inflates cost by $+178.5\%$ ($0.157 \to 0.288$ LPIPS), whereas varying tail growth $r \in [1.4, 3.0]$ changes DP cost by $\le 15.4\%$ and physical LPIPS by $<1.6\%$ (\textbf{$11.6\times$ anisotropy}).

\paragraph{Discovery III: Multiplicative Cross-Prompt Quantile Invariance.}
Across budgets $B \in \{16, 22, 28\}$, single-skip profiles correlate at $\rho = 0.94$ across prompts, and the \emph{worst-to-mean} LPIPS ratio across prompts is **invariant to $1.04\%$ CV** ($0.414/0.253 = 1.636$, $0.259/0.158 = 1.639$, $0.149/0.093 = 1.602$). Naive additive sums ($\beta=0$) fail as certificates ($R^2 = 0.8345$, $29\%$ bound violation), whereas diffusive Bellman cost $\cC_{0.5}$ achieves $\mathbf{R^2 = 0.9847}$ (mean) and $\mathbf{0.9814}$ (worst-case), while 336 online MPC runs alter $0/50$ steps---confirming Theorems~\ref{thm:invariance} and~\ref{thm:certify} (Figure~\ref{fig:theory}d).

\paragraph{Discovery IV: 3D-VAE Spatial Phase Coherence vs.\ Hidden Polynomials \& Token Gates.}
Why does hidden-feature TaylorSeer collapse to $0.4917$ LPIPS ($4.72\times$ worse than \method{}'s $0.1041$)? Our operator decomposition isolates three causes: (1)~Theorem~\ref{thm:shrinkage}'s $\Theta(g^3)$ gap-scaling inflates curvature variance by $k_{\mathrm{rms}}^2 = \mathbf{12.39\times}$ ($0.1041 \to 0.4417$ LPIPS even in output space, explaining $88\%$ of the loss); (2)~internal DiT features rotate across \texttt{AdaLN-Zero} and 3D \texttt{RoPE} layers before output projection; and (3)~across 144 spatial/channel diagnostic runs, per-token adaptive gates (\texttt{pt16c}: $12\%$ win rate, $-21.7\%$ LPIPS) and $64{\times}64$ low-rank channel matrices (\texttt{lr8c}: $0.2418$ vs.\ $0.2375$ global scalar) introduce cross-patch phase tears amplified by 3D causal VAE decoding---explaining why Theorem~\ref{thm:shrinkage}'s spatially uniform 2-slot output scalar dominates.
