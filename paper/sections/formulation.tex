\section{Operator Dynamics and Four Structural Laws}
\label{sec:formulation}

To move beyond empirical trial-and-error, we derive the exact closed-loop perturbation operator of a cached guided DiT and distill four structural laws from $>2{,}600$ GPU trajectory runs that directly prescribe the operators in Section~\ref{sec:method}.

\subsection{Closed-Loop Adjoint Operator Formulation}
\label{sec:bilevel_obj}
Let a pretrained video DiT evolve $\{x_i\}_{i=0}^T \subset \R^d$ across grid $\{\tau_0 > \dots > \tau_T\}$ under prompt $c$ and CFG scale $\omega \ge 1$. Evaluating step $i$ queries both branches for the guided velocity
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
v_i &= f_\omega(x_i, \tau_i, c) \\
&\coloneqq v_\theta(x_i, \tau_i, \varnothing) + \omega \bigl(v_\theta(x_i, \tau_i, c) - v_\theta(x_i, \tau_i, \varnothing)\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:cfg_velocity}
\end{equation}
Deterministic integrators---including DDIM \citep{song2021ddim} on CogVideoX and multistep UniPC \citep{zhao2023unipc} on Wan2.1---advance the state via exact solver bookkeeping
\begin{equation}
x_{i+1} = a_i x_i + b_i \tilde{v}_i, \qquad i = 0, \dots, T - 1,
\label{eq:solver_step}
\end{equation}
where $\tilde{v}_i = v_i$ for $i \in \cS$ and $\tilde{v}_i = \cR(\cB_{s_m}, i)$ on $i \in (s_m, s_{m+1})$, with 2-anchor buffer $\cB_{s_m} = \{(s_{m-1}, v_{s_{m-1}}), (s_m, v_{s_m})\}$.\footnote{For multistep UniPC, writing Eq.~\eqref{eq:solver_step} on augmented history $\bar{x}_i = (x_i, \tilde{v}_{i-1})$ preserves exact affine dynamics.} Let $x_T^\star$ be the 50-step terminal latent, $\cD$ the 3D causal VAE decoder, and $\Phi$ the VGG perceptual map \citep{zhang2018lpips} ($\mathrm{LPIPS}(\hat{y}, y^\star)^2 = \norm{\Phi(\hat{y}) - \Phi(y^\star)}_2^2$). Over boundary-pinned sets $\mathfrak{S}_B \coloneqq \{\cS \subseteq \{0,\dots,T-1\} : |\cS| = B,\; s_0 = 0,\; s_{B-1} = T-1\}$ ($g_m = s_{m+1} - s_m$), caching solves the bilevel problem
\begin{equation}
\min_{\cS \in \mathfrak{S}_B} \min_{\cR \in \mathfrak{R}} \E_{c, x_0}\!\Bigl[\norm{\Phi(\cD(x_T(\cS, \cR))) - \Phi(\cD(x_T^\star))}_2^2\Bigr].
\label{eq:bilevel}
\end{equation}

Linearizing $\delta x_i \coloneqq x_i(\cS, \cR) - x_i^\star$ defines the \textbf{closed-loop step Jacobian} $A_i^{\mathrm{cl}} \coloneqq a_i I + b_i \nabla_x f_\omega(x_i^\star, \tau_i, c)$, \textbf{horizon adjoint} $\Psi_{k \to T} \coloneqq A_{T-1}^{\mathrm{cl}} \cdots A_k^{\mathrm{cl}}$ ($\Psi_{T\to T} = I$), and open-loop weights $\omega_{m,k} \coloneqq (\prod_{\ell=s_m+k+1}^{s_{m+1}-1} a_\ell) b_{s_m+k}$:

\begin{proposition}[Adjoint Horizon Decomposition \& Subspace Barrier]
\label{prop:subspace}
Let $\Pi_{\cB_{s_m}}$ be the orthogonal projector onto $\mathrm{span}\{v : (\cdot, v) \in \cB_{s_m}\}$ and $\Pi_{\cB_{s_m}}^\perp = I - \Pi_{\cB_{s_m}}$. To first order, $\Phi(\cD(x_T)) - \Phi(\cD(x_T^\star)) = J_{\Phi \circ \cD}(x_T^\star)\,\delta x_T$ with
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\delta x_T = \sum_{m=0}^{B-2} \Psi_{s_{m+1} \to T} \sum_{k=1}^{g_m - 1} \omega_{m,k} \bigl(&\cR(\cB_{s_m}, s_m + k) \\[-3pt]
&- v_{s_m+k}^\star\bigr),
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:adjoint_sum}
\end{equation}
and for \emph{any} linear predictor $\cR_{\mathrm{lin}}(\cB_{s_m}, j) \in \mathrm{span}(\cB_{s_m})$ (including hindsight least-squares oracles),
\begin{equation}
\inf_{\cR_{\mathrm{lin}} \in \mathrm{span}(\cB_{s_m})} \norm{\cR_{\mathrm{lin}}(\cB_{s_m}, j) - v_j^\star}_2 = \norm{\Pi_{\cB_{s_m}}^\perp v_j^\star}_2.
\label{eq:subspace_floor}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\subsection{Four Sharp Laws and Design Principles}
\label{sec:laws}
Evaluating Eqs.~\eqref{eq:adjoint_sum}--\eqref{eq:subspace_floor} across controlled diagnostic suites on CogVideoX-2B and Wan2.1-1.3B (Appendix~\ref{app:proof_prop1} and Section~\ref{sec:exp_theory}) reveals four counter-intuitive laws, each prescribing a core mechanism of \method{}:

\paragraph{Law I: Two-Regime Bifurcation \& Sign-Inversion $\implies$ Phase-Gated Non-Commutativity.}
Guided trajectories bifurcate sharply at $H_{\mathrm{crit}}$ (steps $10\text{--}12$ at $T=30$, $14\text{--}16$ at $T=50$; Figure~\ref{fig:theory}a): in \emph{Regime~I ($i < H_{\mathrm{crit}}$)}, consecutive chords rotate nearly orthogonally ($\cos\theta_5 = \mathbf{0.0959}$, i.e., $\mathbf{84.5^\circ}$!; innovation $1.088$) while closed-loop gain peaks at $\gamma_3 = \mathbf{0.9630}$; in \emph{Regime~II ($i \ge H_{\mathrm{crit}}$)}, chord alignment recovers $\mathbf{5.20\times}$ ($0.0959 \to 0.4988$) while Jacobian gain contracts $\mathbf{8.11\times}$ ($0.9630 \to 0.1188$; ratio-of-means $6.42\times$), whereas solver-only $c_0$ moves backwards ($0.85\times$). \emph{Implication:} $\cS$ and $\cR$ \textbf{do not commute} (Figure~\ref{fig:theory}b). Across 576 runs, 1st-order extrapolation \emph{hurts} by $\mathbf{+31.0\%}$ ($0.2810 \to 0.3681$ rel-$L_2$) when the head is skipped and uniform grids trap 3-anchor least-squares at $0.4218$; yet locking $[0, H_{\mathrm{crit}})$ flips the \emph{exact same} chord to $\mathbf{-42.2\%}$ ($0.1568 \to 0.0907$), motivating both head saturation and inner phase gating $\mathbb{I}[i \ge H_{\mathrm{crit}}]$.

\paragraph{Law II: Input Anti-Observability, Rank Inversion, \& Terminal Blackout $\implies$ Boundary-Pinned Closed-Loop Pricing.}
At strictly matched 19 calls on CogVideoX-2B, TeaCache's input-modulation delta yields $0.2320$ LPIPS vs.\ $\mathbf{0.0523}$ for output drift (\textbf{$4.44\times$ worse}; our 10-call schedule at $0.1229$ beats 22-call input thresholding at $0.2543$). Across 160 multi-gap runs, open-loop solver priors \emph{invert Spearman rank} with terminal error from $+0.545$ ($g=2$) to $\mathbf{-0.148}$ ($g=6$), while cumulative drift retains $+0.909$ and compounds $6.56\times$ (Figure~\ref{fig:theory}c). Across 699 Wan2.1 UniPC runs, bypassing Eq.~\eqref{eq:solver_step} collapses LPIPS to $0.83$, and unpinned greedy schedules suffer a $4.56\times$ terminal blackout ($0.3295$ vs.\ $0.0723$ rel-$L_2$). \emph{Implication:} Placement must price closed-loop impulse $w_j = \mathrm{LPIPS}(\cD(x_T(\cS_{-j}, \cR_0)), \cD(x_T^\star))$ ($\tilde{w}_j = \max(w_j - \min_k w_k, \eps_0)$) from one $\mathcal{O}(T)$ sweep, preserve solver bookkeeping, and pin $s_{B-1} = T - 1$.

\paragraph{Law III: Multiplicative Cross-Prompt Scaling $\implies$ Offline Collapse \& Conformal Budget Synthesis.}
Across $B \in \{16, 22, 28\}$, single-skip profiles correlate at $\rho = 0.94$ and the worst-to-mean LPIPS ratio across prompts is **invariant to $1.04\%$ CV** ($1.636, 1.639, 1.602$). Distortion factors as $\mathrm{LPIPS}(\cS, \alpha; c) \approx \kappa(c)\,\rho(\alpha)\,\cC_{0.5}(\cS)$, where $\cC_{0.5}$ achieves $R^2 = \mathbf{0.9847}$ (mean) / $\mathbf{0.9814}$ (worst) vs.\ $0.8345$ for additive $\beta=0$ ($29\%$ bound violation). \emph{Implication:} $\kappa(c) > 0$ cancels under fixed $B$, while its stable upper quantile enables inverse budget certification $B^\star(\eps, \delta, \alpha)$ (Figure~\ref{fig:theory}d).

\paragraph{Law IV: Output-Tangent Spatial Coherence $\implies$ 2-Slot Scalar Shrinkage.}
Hidden-feature TaylorSeer collapses to $0.4917$ LPIPS ($4.72\times$ worse than our $0.1041$) for three reasons: (1)~scaling by step age $(j - s_m)$ inflates curvature variance by $k_{\mathrm{rms}}^2 = \mathbf{12.39\times}$ ($0.1041 \to 0.4417$ even in output space); (2)~hidden features rotate across \texttt{AdaLN-Zero} and 3D \texttt{RoPE}; and (3)~across 144 runs, per-token gates (\texttt{pt16c}: $12\%$ win rate) and $64{\times}64$ channel maps (\texttt{lr8c}: $0.2418$ vs.\ $0.2375$ scalar) induce inter-patch phase tears in 3D VAE decoding. \emph{Implication:} Use a spatially uniform scalar $\alpha$ in guided output-tangent space with only 2 cached tensors.
