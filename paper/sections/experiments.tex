\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

Without re-running or altering any benchmark setup, we evaluate \method{} across five dimensions: (1)~100-prompt end-to-end fidelity across backbones and solvers; (2)~direct verification of our four structural laws across $>2{,}600$ GPU trajectory runs; (3)~conformal risk-certified budget synthesis (\texttt{CertifyBudget}, Theorem~\ref{thm:certify}); (4)~phase-gated midpoint shrinkage vs.\ polynomial and spatial/channel alternatives (Theorem~\ref{thm:shrinkage}); and (5)~stiff-sloppy boundary ablations and wall-clock speedups.

\subsection{Experimental Protocol}
\label{sec:exp_setup}
Every comparison table executes all competing arms inside a \emph{single} codebase against a \emph{single} uncached 50-step reference with paired prompts and seeds (Appendix~\ref{app:credibility}). Cross-method benchmarks on CogVideoX-2B \citep{yang2025cogvideox} (49f and 17f, $720{\times}480$, DDIM-50) and Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B \citep{wan2025wan} (81f, $832{\times}480$, 2nd-order UniPC-50) run inside the official RA-CFGCache harness \citep{liu2026racfgcache} over 100 VBench prompts \citep{huang2024vbench}, injecting $\cS^\star$ via one step hook while leaving CFG, solver bookkeeping, and causal 3D VAE decoding identical. Inner-operator sweeps run in native \texttt{diffusers} under fixed 22-call $\cS^\star$. Each dual-branch CFG pass counts as 1 call.

\subsection{Stiff-Sloppy Ablations and Wall-Clock Speedup}
\label{sec:exp_ablations}
Expanding head allocation $H \in \{6, 12, 16\}$ drops 17f LPIPS from $0.288 \to 0.157 \to \mathbf{0.133}$ (Table~\ref{tab:head}) and 49f LPIPS ($n=100$) from $0.1424 \to \mathbf{0.1300}$, whereas sweeping interior tail ratio $r \in [1.4, 3.0]$ stays within $0.1541\text{--}0.1566$ (Appendix~\ref{app:shape}) and TaylorSeer remains $\ge 0.402$ even at 26 calls (Table~\ref{tab:taylor}). On an NVIDIA H20 GPU ($2.70$~s/call on CogVideoX-2B 49f; $6.50$~s/call on Wan2.1 81f), \method{} accelerates 49f generation from $135$~s to $54\text{--}59$~s ($2.3\text{--}2.5\times$, or $2.6\times$ at certified $B^\star=19$) with $<2$~ms total CPU/GPU overhead and only 2 cached output tensors.
