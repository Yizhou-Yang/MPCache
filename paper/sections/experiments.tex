\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

We evaluate \method{} across four questions at matched transformer budgets: (1)~end-to-end fidelity against state-of-the-art training-free caches across backbones and frame counts; (2)~direct verification of Proposition~\ref{prop:subspace} and Theorems~\ref{thm:kkt}--\ref{thm:invariance} on 928 GPU trajectory runs; (3)~verification of Theorem~\ref{thm:shrinkage}'s Hilbert quadratic risk law and midpoint chord-shrinkage; and (4)~sensitivity to head length, tail shape, and budget.

\subsection{Experimental Protocol}
\label{sec:exp_setup}
Every comparison table runs all competing arms inside a \emph{single} codebase against a \emph{single} uncached 50-step reference with paired prompts and seeds (Appendix~\ref{app:credibility}). Cross-method evaluations on CogVideoX-2B \citep{yang2025cogvideox} (49f and 17f, $720\times 480$, 50 DDIM steps) and Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B \citep{wan2025wan} (81f, $832\times 480$, 50 UniPC steps) execute inside the official RA-CFGCache harness \citep{liu2026racfgcache} over 100 VBench prompts \citep{huang2024vbench}, injecting $\cS^\star$ through one step-decision hook while keeping CFG, solver updates, and decoding untouched. Inner-operator predictor sweeps (Section~\ref{sec:exp_shrinkage}) execute inside native \texttt{diffusers} under the identical 22-call schedule. We report VGG-LPIPS \citep{zhang2018lpips}, SSIM, and PSNR, counting each dual-branch CFG evaluation as one call.

Figure~\ref{fig:theory} and Table~\ref{tab:theory_verif} verify each theoretical claim directly on ground-truth GPU traces: (1)~\emph{Adjoint Damping (192 runs):} true closed-loop gain contracts $6.42\times$ ($0.765 \to 0.119$, terminal error $25.2\times$) due to exact denoiser Jacobians $\nabla_x f_\omega$, whereas solver-only $c_0$ moves opposite ($0.85\times$); (2)~\emph{Subspace Barrier (576 runs):} on uniform $N=17$ grids, step-1 orthogonal innovation ($1.088$) traps hindsight-optimal 3-anchor `lsq` at $0.4218$ rel-$L_2$ ($\approx 0.4239$ reuse), whereas head-dense placement cuts error $3.5\times$ to $0.1205$ and unlocks linear prediction ($0.0924$); (3)~\emph{Diffusive Age DP (160 runs):} $\cC_{0.5}$ raises cross-budget LPIPS $R^2$ from $0.835$ to $\mathbf{0.985}$, predicts 5-skip compounding ($6.50$ vs.\ $6.56$), and bounds max tail gap at $9$ (vs.\ $29$ for $\beta=0$); (4)~\emph{MPC Collapse:} receding-horizon replanning alters $0/50$ steps relative to offline DP.

Holding the 22-call schedule fixed, Table~\ref{tab:reuse} and Figure~\ref{fig:damping} validate Theorem~\ref{thm:shrinkage}: midpoint shrinkage ($\alpha=0.5$) reduces 49-frame LPIPS by $\mathbf{32.8\%}$ ($0.1549 \to \mathbf{0.1041}$) and boosts PSNR by $\mathbf{+3.70}$~dB ($31.25$~dB) at zero extra cost (and reaches $\mathbf{0.1084}$ vs.\ $0.1556$ at 17 frames). Fitting Eq.~\eqref{eq:hilbert_parabola} yields $\mathbf{R^2 = 0.9974}$ ($\alpha^\star = 0.650$) on 49f and $\mathbf{R^2 = 0.9868}$ ($\alpha^\star = 0.623$) on 17f, matching the theoretical midpoint estimate $\alpha_{\mathrm{mid}}^\star \in [0.55, 0.62]$. Conversely, gap-scaled $(j-s)\Delta_s$ collapses to $0.4417$ LPIPS ($4.24\times$ worse), quantitatively matching $k_{\mathrm{rms}}(\cS^\star) = 3.52$.

\subsection{Schedule Ablations and Runtime Speedup}
\label{sec:exp_ablations}
Varying head length $H \in \{6, 12, 16\}$ at $B \approx 20\text{--}22$ (Table~\ref{tab:head}) drops 17f LPIPS from $0.288 \to 0.157 \to \mathbf{0.133}$ and 49f LPIPS ($n=100$) from $0.1424 \to \mathbf{0.1300}$, validating $H^\star(22)=16$. Varying tail growth $r \in \{1.0, 1.4, 2.0, 3.0\}$ at fixed $H=12, B=20$ improves LPIPS by $5\text{--}7\%$ over uniform tail spacing ($0.1656 \to 0.1541$) and is flat across $r \in [1.4, 3.0]$ (Appendix~\ref{app:shape}), while TaylorSeer stays above $0.402$ LPIPS even at 26 calls (Table~\ref{tab:taylor}). On one NVIDIA H20 GPU ($2.70$~s/call on CogVideoX-2B 49f; $6.50$~s/call on Wan2.1 81f), \method{} cuts 49f denoiser latency from $135$~s to $54\text{--}59$~s ($2.3\text{--}2.5\times$ speedup) with $<2$~ms total runtime overhead.
