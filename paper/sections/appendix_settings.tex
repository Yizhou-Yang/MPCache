\subsection{Extended Trace Breakdowns of the Four Structural Laws}
\label{app:extended_traces}

Table~\ref{tab:trace_laws} documents the exact quantitative measurements from the controlled GPU trajectory datasets analyzed in Section~\ref{sec:discoveries} and Table~\ref{tab:theory_verif}:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{576-Run Oracle Matrix (\texttt{step\_oracle\_20261015}):} Crosses 5 outer placement policies (\texttt{uniform}, \texttt{teacache}, \texttt{head\_uniform}, \texttt{oracle\_greedy}, \texttt{othr}) with 4 inner reconstruction operators (\texttt{reuse}, \texttt{ex1}, \texttt{ab2}, and hindsight 3-anchor least-squares \texttt{lsq}) on CogVideoX-2B ($T=30$). Without head protection (\texttt{teacache} $N=10$), \texttt{ex1} degrades rel-$L_2$ from $0.2810 \to 0.3681$ ($+31.0\%$), whereas under \texttt{othr} ($\tau=0.16$) \texttt{ex1} slashes rel-$L_2$ from $0.1568 \to 0.0907$ ($-42.2\%$).
\item \textbf{Matched 19-Call Signal Ablation (\texttt{SIGNAL\_ABLATION\_20261031}):} Holding budget ($B=19$), step quota rule, and solver identical on 50-step CogVideoX-2B, TeaCache's modulated-input delta yields $0.2320$ LPIPS vs.\ $0.0523$ for output drift ($4.44\times$ better).
\item \textbf{699-Run Wan2.1 UniPC Anatomy (\texttt{wan33\_20261022a}):} Demonstrates both solver-bookkeeping necessity (direct latent extrapolation \texttt{lx} / inversion \texttt{inv} collapses LPIPS to $0.831$) and terminal boundary pinning (unpinned greedy \texttt{mpc} stops at step $27/39$ for $N=10/13$ with $0.4493 / 0.3295$ rel-$L_2$, whereas pinning $T-1=49$ drops error to $0.1933 / 0.1247$, and reaching step $48$ at $N=17$ drops error $4.56\times$ to $0.0723$).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Stiff Head vs.\ Sloppy Tail Growth Sensitivity}
\label{app:shape}
Fixing $H = 12$ and $B = 20$ on CogVideoX-2B (17 frames, 32 prompts) and varying only the geometric tail growth ratio $r \in \{1.0, 1.4, 2.0, 3.0\}$ yields $0.1656$ LPIPS ($27.44$~dB PSNR) for a uniform tail ($r=1.0$), compared to $0.1560$ ($28.61$~dB), $0.1566$ ($28.32$~dB), and $\mathbf{0.1541}$ ($29.08$~dB) at $r = 1.4, 2.0, 3.0$ ($<1.6\%$ variation across $r \in [1.4, 3.0]$), empirically confirming Corollary~\ref{cor:sloppy}.
