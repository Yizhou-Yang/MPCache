\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Video Diffusion Transformers (DiTs) achieve high spatiotemporal fidelity by integrating a learned velocity or noise-prediction field across $T \approx 50$ sequential solver steps \citep{peebles2023dit,yang2025cogvideox,wan2025wan,kong2024hunyuanvideo}. Under classifier-free guidance (CFG) \citep{ho2022cfg}, every step requires both a conditional and an unconditional forward pass through billions of parameters over tens of thousands of video tokens, making inference latency the primary bottleneck in deployment. Training-free caching mitigates this cost without modifying model weights or solver grids: a subset of $B < T$ anchor steps is evaluated exactly, while the remaining $T - B$ steps substitute an inexpensive approximation constructed from cached evaluations \citep{ma2024deepcache,liu2025teacache,liu2025taylorseer,liu2026racfgcache}.

Despite rapid empirical progress, existing caching methods treat \emph{when to evaluate} and \emph{what to return} as decoupled heuristics, leaving three foundational questions unresolved:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Local vs.\ Closed-Loop Horizon Pricing:} Online threshold caches---including TeaCache \citep{liu2025teacache}, SenCache \citep{haghighi2026sencache}, and RA-CFGCache \citep{liu2026racfgcache}---decide step-by-step using local input drift, local denoiser Jacobians, or instantaneous branch risk. Yet a caching perturbation injected at step $j$ does not reach the decoded video directly; it is recursively transformed by $T - 1 - j$ coupled solver-denoiser transitions. Meanwhile, path-planning methods such as DPCache \citep{cui2026dpcache} and IdealCache \citep{xue2026idealcache} require $\mathcal{O}(N_{\mathrm{cal}} T^2)$ multi-sample rollout evaluations or ad-hoc mandatory prefixes (e.g., fixing the first $M=3$ steps) without an analytical law governing head allocation.
\item \textbf{Open-Loop Planning vs.\ Online Feedback:} Recent works invoke dynamic thresholds, reinforcement learning, or model-predictive control to adapt schedules per prompt \citep{haghighi2026sencache,li2026racer}. Under a fixed call budget $B$, when does online trajectory feedback genuinely alter the optimal schedule, and when does closed-loop control collapse mathematically to a zero-overhead offline plan?
\item \textbf{Extrapolation Instability on Curved Manifolds:} Beyond plain zeroth-order reuse ($\tilde{v}_j = v_s$), feature-forecasting methods such as TaylorSeer \citep{liu2025taylorseer} and ERTACache \citep{peng2025ertacache} extrapolate across a skip of age $k = j - s$ via gap-scaled polynomials ($\tilde{v}_{s+k} = v_s + k \Delta_s + \cdots$). On multi-second video backbones such as CogVideoX-2B, TaylorSeer degrades severely ($0.492$ LPIPS vs.\ $0.159$ for plain-reuse risk caching at 19 calls), even when given $30\%$ more calls than competing schedules. Why does textbook Taylor extrapolation fail, and what causal operator is optimal over non-uniform gaps?
\end{enumerate}

In this paper, we resolve all three questions within a unified mathematical and algorithmic framework, \textbf{\method{}}, which formulates training-free video diffusion caching as a \emph{bilevel variational operator-design problem} (Figure~\ref{fig:teaser}). At the outer level, a horizon placement operator $\cS \in \mathfrak{S}_B$ partitions $\{0,\dots,T-1\}$ into $B-1$ intervals $[s_m, s_{m+1})$ to suppress closed-loop terminal amplification; at the inner level, a causal two-anchor reconstruction operator $\cR_\alpha$ minimizes solver-integrated quadrature drift across each skipped interval. Our contributions are fourfold:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Adjoint Horizon Decomposition \& Subspace Barrier (Section~\ref{sec:formulation}, Proposition~\ref{prop:subspace}):} Linearizing the discrete guided solver-denoiser loop yields an exact horizon transition operator $\Psi_{k\to T}$. Across 768 GPU diagnostic runs, we show that true closed-loop error gain decays $6.4\times$ from head to tail---whereas solver-only priors ignoring the denoiser Jacobian $\nabla_x f_\omega$ move in the \emph{opposite} direction---and that orthogonal high-noise velocity innovations create an irreducible subspace floor ($0.422$ rel-$L_2$) on uniform grids that defeats even hindsight-optimal least-squares predictors.
\item \textbf{Diffusive Age-Weighted Bellman DP \& KKT Head-Saturation Law (Section~\ref{sec:dp}, Theorem~\ref{thm:kkt}):} Using only a single $\mathcal{O}(T)$ terminal impulse pass $\{w_j\}$, we account for anchor-age drift ($j - s_m \ge 1$) via a parameter-free diffusive compounding functional $\cC_\beta(\cS)$ ($\beta=1/2$), improving cross-budget LPIPS prediction from $R^2=0.835$ (naive additivity) to $R^2=0.985$ and matching 5-step tail compounding ($6.50$ predicted vs.\ $6.56$ measured). Solving $\min_{|\cS|=B} \cC_\beta(\cS)$ globally via $\mathcal{O}(BT^2)$ Bellman dynamic programming and analyzing its continuous KKT relaxation proves that optimal schedules saturate a contiguous exact head $\{0,\dots,H^\star(B)-1\}$ whose length grows monotonically with $B$ ($H^\star(22)=16$, far larger than ad-hoc $M=3$ prefixes), followed by strictly growing tail gaps.
\item \textbf{Scale-Invariance Collapse of Receding-Horizon Control (Section~\ref{sec:invariance}, Theorem~\ref{thm:invariance}):} We prove that whenever prompt-specific trajectory difficulty enters as a positive multiplicative drift scale $\hat{\sigma}_k(x_{0:s_k}) > 0$ under a hard call budget $B$, the receding-horizon Bellman minimizer is strictly invariant to $\{\hat{\sigma}_k\}$ at every step and collapses identically to the offline DP schedule $\cS^\star$. Because empirical impulse profiles preserve step rank across prompts (Spearman $\rho = 0.94$), zero-overhead offline scheduling achieves near-oracle placement.
\item \textbf{Causal Midpoint Chord-Shrinkage \& Hilbert Risk Law (Section~\ref{sec:shrinkage}, Theorem~\ref{thm:shrinkage}):} Conditioning on $\cS^\star$, we introduce the gap-invariant two-anchor operator $\cR_\alpha(\cB_s, j) = v_s + \alpha(v_s - v_{s'})$. We prove that expected squared distortion in perceptual Hilbert space is an exact convex parabola $\mathcal{E}(\alpha) = \mathcal{E}_{\min} + c_2(\alpha - \alpha^\star)^2$ ($R^2 = 0.997$ at 49 frames, $R^2 = 0.987$ at 17 frames) minimized at the \emph{midpoint quadrature gain} $\alpha^\star \approx 1/2$, which cancels first-order solver drift across each interval while avoiding the $\mathcal{O}(g^3)$ curvature variance inflation ($12.4\times$ squared-error blow-up) of gap-scaled extrapolation.
\end{itemize}

Evaluated under strict single-harness protocols across CogVideoX-2B (49 frames and 17 frames) and Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B (81 frames) on 100 VBench prompts, \method{} consistently outperforms TeaCache, TaylorSeer, and RA-CFGCache across LPIPS, SSIM, and PSNR at matched budgets, while midpoint chord-shrinkage delivers an additional $32.8\%$ LPIPS reduction ($+3.70$~dB PSNR) with zero runtime latency or memory overhead beyond a single cached tensor.
